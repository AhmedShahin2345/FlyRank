# Week 3 · The Through-Line — Map Content & CTAs

## The one-line claim

Ten AI options, then the pick:

> "I turn campus energy into sponsor-ready ROI."

Chosen over: "student marketing that speaks Gen Z", "data-driven campaigns for
the campus crowd", "from student union to sponsor boardroom", etc. Those all
describe a service; the winner describes a *transformation with a business
result* (campus energy → sponsor-ready ROI) and ends on the number language a
Director of Youth Marketing actually cares about. Sharpened from the Week 1
proof statement: shorter, past-tense energy, no jargon.

## Content map

Every page sends the visitor one step closer to the single action:
**contact me to lead Gen-Z focused marketing strategy or experiential
campaigns.**

### 1. Home / Landing — the claim, then the proof
1. Hero: one-line claim + the real portrait + CTA button "See the work"
2. Proof strip: 2–3 real numbers (ticket-sale conversion, reach, sponsor ROI)
3. Featured case: SU Ticket Sales (the strongest — leads)
4. CTA: "Email me about a campaign like this"
5. Footer: name, email, LinkedIn

### 2. The Work / Case Studies — lead with the strongest
1. Case 1 — SU Ticket Sales (data-driven social + conversion): context, the
   before/after screenshots, the numbers, what I'd do bigger
   CTA: "I want ticket numbers like this"
2. Case 2 — AUC Welcome Party (experiential + creative theme): photos, the
   activation, sponsor value delivered
   CTA: "I want an activation like this"
3. Internship proof strip: the live dev work (API, scraper, LLM endpoint
   links) — shows I can also *build* the tools that measure campaigns
   CTA: "See the code" → GitHub

### 3. About / My Process — trust, not biography
1. Short: AUC SU Marketing Head, student-marketer-operator
2. Process: measure → translate → execute (how campus chaos becomes data)
3. Portrait (same real photo)
4. CTA: "Work with me"

### 4. Contact — zero friction
1. Form: name, email, company, "what do you want to build"
2. CTA: "Send it" (plus a mailto fallback so the action survives any form bug)
