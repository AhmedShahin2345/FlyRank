# Week 3 · Identity Kit — Decide Once

One page. Four decisions, made once, inherited by every page and case study.

## Fonts

- **Heading — Fraunces** (Google Fonts, free, OFL). A soft "old-style" serif with
  personality — it carries the editorial, youth-culture energy without shouting.
- **Body — Inter** (Google Fonts, free, OFL). Neutral, legible, modern. It does
  the work of carrying numbers, captions, and reading text while staying quiet.

One display face + one workhorse. Nothing else gets loaded.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Main | `#0F4C5C` | Deep teal — the "sponsor's navy" energy, used for headings, buttons, the monogram |
| Near-black | `#17181A` | Body text, captions |
| Near-white | `#FAFAF7` | Background — warm, not sterile white |
| Accent (one) | `#E07A5F` | Muted coral — reserved for a single underline, a stat, one CTA per page |

Four colors. The accent is used at most once per viewport.

## Logo / favicon

`identity/as-monogram.svg` — a rounded square in the main teal with the
monogram **AS** set in the heading face, near-white. Used as the favicon and as
the corner mark on every page. Simple enough to survive at 16×16 px.

![AS monogram](identity/as-monogram.svg)

## Style note (two lines — goes into the Claude Project)

> Editorial warmth, quiet precision: Fraunces headlines + Inter body on warm
> near-white, deep teal `#0F4C5C` as the single structural color, one muted
> coral `#E07A5F` accent per page. The work and the numbers are the loudest
> things on every page — the design frames them, never upstages them.

## Why these, in one sentence

The kit had to feel like a student-marketing operator who can walk into a
sponsor meeting: warm enough to be human, structured enough to be trusted,
and calm enough that campaign screenshots and ROI numbers stay the loudest
element on the page.
